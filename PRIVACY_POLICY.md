# CascadeZero — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

CascadeZero is a disruption-response tool for manufacturing operations. It is currently a
demonstration / pilot project. This policy explains what data the CascadeZero WhatsApp
integration processes and why.

## What we collect

When you exchange WhatsApp messages with the CascadeZero business number, we receive from
Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform:

- your WhatsApp phone number and profile name;
- the content of messages you send us (for example a disruption alert, a supplier's reply to
  an enquiry, or a button you tap such as "Approve");
- message delivery status (sent, delivered, read).

We do not collect payment details, location, contacts, or any data from outside the
conversation with our business number.

## How we use it

- **Disruption alerts** — to extract delay, shortage and machine-status figures and compute
  recovery options.
- **Supplier enquiries** — to read a supplier's offer (quantity, price, delivery time) and
  update the recovery plan.
- **Approvals** — to record which authorised phone number approved a plan (audit trail).

Messages may be processed by Google's Gemini API to extract structured figures from free text.
Numbers and decisions are computed by our own software; a human approves every plan.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone other than the
service providers needed to run the system (Meta WhatsApp Business Platform, Google Gemini API,
Supabase database hosting).

## Retention

Messages and the records derived from them (disruption events, agent logs, enquiries, draft
purchase orders) are kept for as long as the pilot runs and are deleted when the pilot ends, or
earlier on request.

## Your choices

You can stop receiving messages at any time by replying "STOP" or by blocking the number.
To request access to or deletion of your data, email the contact below.
