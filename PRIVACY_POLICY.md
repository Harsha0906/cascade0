# CascadeZero — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

CascadeZero is a disruption-response tool for manufacturing operations. It is currently a
demonstration / pilot project. This policy explains what data the CascadeZero web dashboard and
Telegram bot process and why.

## What we collect

- **Account data** — the email address you sign up with, your role in a factory (owner,
  approver, viewer) and which factory you belong to.
- **Factory data you enter or import** — production lines, suppliers, stock, shipments and
  customer orders (typed in or imported from Tally/Excel files).
- **Telegram messages** — when you message the CascadeZero Telegram bot we receive your Telegram
  chat id and name and the content of your messages (for example a disruption alert, a
  supplier's reply to an enquiry, or a button you tap such as "Approve"). A chat is connected to
  an account or a supplier/customer only after its owner sends a one-time link code or taps an
  invite link.

We do not collect payment details, location or contacts, and we never read Telegram
conversations other than those with our bot.

## How we use it

- **Disruption alerts** — to extract delay, shortage and machine-status figures and compute
  recovery options.
- **Supplier enquiries** — to send enquiries (never orders) and read a supplier's offer
  (quantity, price, delivery time) to update the recovery plan.
- **Customer requests** — to send a deadline-extension request only after a person approves it.
- **Approvals** — to record which signed-in user approved a plan (audit trail).

Messages may be processed by Google's Gemini API to extract structured figures from free text.
Numbers and decisions are computed by our own software; a human approves every plan.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone other than the
service providers needed to run the system (Telegram, Google Gemini API, Supabase database and
authentication, Render and Vercel hosting). Each factory's data is visible only to that
factory's members.

## Retention

Records (disruption events, agent logs, enquiries, draft purchase orders) are kept for as long as
the pilot runs and are deleted when the pilot ends, or earlier on request.

## Your choices

You can stop receiving bot messages at any time by blocking the bot in Telegram. To request
access to or deletion of your data, email the contact below.
